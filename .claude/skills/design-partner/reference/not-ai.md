# Owen's "doesn't look AI-made" list

This adds to the `impeccable` skill's absolute bans and font reflex-reject
list (see its `reference/brand.md`). Owen asked for these specifically.

- **No eyebrow over every heading.** Use a small label above a heading only
  where it carries information the heading doesn't. At most one or two per
  page, and never as a section-by-section habit.
- **No default "AI fonts".** Besides impeccable's list, treat Geist,
  Manrope, Poppins, Montserrat and system-UI-for-everything as reflexes.
  They're fine when chosen for a reason, never as a default. Find type in a
  real foundry or catalog. Owen's own site already uses Poppins and
  Montserrat (see docs/STYLE-GUIDE.md). Keep them there unless he decides
  otherwise. That's an open question in TASTE.md.
- **No template skeleton.** Avoid hero, then three icon cards, then a
  testimonial carousel, then a CTA band, then a footer, with every section
  centred.
- **No decorative filler.** Blurry gradient blobs, sparkle icons, purple-to-
  blue gradients, icons in pastel circles, emoji as icons, and stock-photo
  handshakes.
- **No identical rounded cards with soft shadows** as the answer to every
  grouping.
- **No buzzword copy** ("elevate", "unlock", "seamless", "journey",
  "empower"). Owen's voice rules in CLAUDE.md apply to any copy.

What works instead: a clear typographic hierarchy, asymmetry where it helps
the reading order, real photography or bespoke illustration, one memorable
detail per page (Owen's signature, see TASTE.md), and generous but uneven
space.
